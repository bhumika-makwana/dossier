Strata: Mechanistically informed model structures for transcriptomics perturbation effect prediction [Ongoing]

Developing a new attention architecture for predicting cellular responses to genetic perturbations. Strata makes biological structure a learnable component of attention, rather than a fixed input, allowing the model to discover and revise regulatory relationships directly from perturbational data. The research explores whether a different inductive bias can help models represent long-range, multi-step information flow in biological systems, rather than learning correlations between genes in isolation. The broader goal is to develop new model structures from first principles that better capture the rules of cellular systems and generalize to biological configurations unseen during training.

Ener'gentic Hackathon 2025 Winner [Aug'25]

View demo →

Organised by Beckn, with Google India and Antler, presented at Stanford University.

India is adding renewables at one of the fastest rates in the world, but our grids still struggle with peak demand surges, costly backup power, and underutilised clean energy. Towards an effort to solve for this, we built GridSense — a multi-agent demand response marketplace for residential and small commercial energy users, powered by AI. The system discovers flexible loads, reasons over dynamic grid signals, negotiates bids, and automates load adjustments across transaction systems.

Democratizing AI to ensure an open, safe, and equitable future [June'25]

With Project NANDA, under Prof. Ramesh Raskar (MIT Media Lab)

Built selective context retrieval and stateful tool execution for LLM agents over MCP. The server exposes persistent WhatsApp conversations as a tool-accessible environment, enabling LLM agents to retrieve relevant historical context, perform stateful messaging actions, process multimodal messages, and operate concurrently across independent conversational sessions.

Multi-channel SME chatbot with self-healing knowledge graphs [March'25 – May'25]

Built Polymorph, an agent platform for persona-driven LLMs deployed across web and WhatsApp. Designed the agent harness around a stateful, self-updating knowledge graph, continuously ingesting business documents, FAQs, and conversation logs to update context and refresh personas. The architecture incorporated self-learning loops for agents to adapt as underlying knowledge and user interactions changed.
