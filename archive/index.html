Ener'gentic Hackathon 2025 Winner

View demo →

organised by Beckn, with Google India and Antler.

India is adding renewables at one of the fastest rates in the world, but our grids still struggle with peak demand surges, costly backup power, and underutilised clean energy. Towards an effort to solve for this, we built Gridsense - an agentic demand response marketplace for residential and small commercial energy users, powered by AI. Built on the open Beckn Protocol and powered by Google Cloud’s Agent Development Kit(ADK).
