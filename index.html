Hi! I'm Bhumika. At 27, I operate out of Bengaluru, India - a city renowned for its rich heritage, vibrant culture, and thriving entrepreneurial ecosystem.

I work to bring AI to science.

I am a Grand Master in ALOHA, and I have loved mathematics since childhood. The tinkering started with an idea to prove mathematics as the language of the universe after reading a metaphor by the astronomer Galileo Galilei, teaching bees to manipulate abstract concepts to solve the arithmetic of a maze - honeybee cognition.

In January 2016, when astronomers found the first physical evidence of a “burping” black hole. That same year, I joined COEP (the third oldest college in Asia), where I was part of Team CSAT, which launched Swayam, making it India's first university-built satellite. In the year 2021, my playground involved vector maps, graph algorithms, and geospatial optimization. I was building a multi-parameter routing algorithm that eventually became an external GSoC project contributing to Google Maps.

I joined GalaxEye (an early stage space tech startup out of IIT Madras) straight out of college as a Founding Engineer. I am extremely proud of what we accomplished together - The World's first multisensor satellite. I developed the algorithm for syncfused optosar. The mission was realised with its first launch onboard SpaceX Falcon CAS500-2 in 2026. It was widely recognised by GOI and received appreciation from Elon Musk and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Later, I continued my pursuit with AI at a stealth startup founded by Ex-Google and Amazon Engineers, alongside contributors from top research labs, to build emotionally intelligent video agents for personalised coaching.

Around the same time, Entrepreneur's First reached out to me. I had been thinking about building something of my own for a while, so I decided to take the leap and started my own venture Pax as many say, with a simple belief:Every downstream decision of architecture, mass, cost, inherited the shape of the fairing. Once you remove that constraint, you unlock entirely different classes of systems.

I now do angel investing as part of Hustle Fund to help make obsession fundable.

I run RareEarth (WIP), a series of dialogues with scientists, technologists, founders, builders, leaders, and visionaries at the intersection of AI × Bio × Singularity. Parallely, exploring my thesis on reprogramming resilience in living beings (biological memory) - for the life on Earth and Beyond.

I’m drawn to interesting technical challenges, ambitious ideas, and teams that enjoy figuring things out together. Reach out if you want to find a way to work together!
